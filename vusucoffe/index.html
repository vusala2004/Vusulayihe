Our Story

Last Summer is a gripping Turkish drama series that aired on Fox TV in 2021. The story centers on idealistic public prosecutor Selim Kara, who takes the son of a crime boss — a young man named Akgün — under his protection in exchange for the father's testimony against organized crime. Kureansiklopedi Akgün moves into Selim's home in İzmir's Çeşme district, where he gradually becomes part of the family and begins to transform his life. Turkish Drama The series masterfully weaves together themes of crime, justice, redemption, and love — especially through the slow-burn romance between Akgün and Selim's daughter Yağmur. Starring Ali Atay and Alperen Duymaz, the show earned an IMDb rating of 8.0 IMDb and left a lasting impression on viewers worldwide.
